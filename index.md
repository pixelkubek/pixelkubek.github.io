# Chess openings

Chess openings are a vital part of a chess game. Choosing a correct gambit can dictate the rest of your game. That's why the have been studied compehensivelly.

In my opinion everyone should know at least a few of them in order to supprise your opponent or to defend against their gambit.

Of course just reading about them is not nearly enough, but its an important starting point.
