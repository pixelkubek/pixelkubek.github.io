## [Fried Liver Attack - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fried_Liver_Attack)
The Fried Liver Attack, also called the Fegatello Attack (named after an Italian dish), is a chess opening. This opening is a variation of the Two Knights Defense in which White sacrifices a knight for an attack on Black's king.

## [How To Play the Fried Liver Attack - Chessable](https://www.chessable.com/blog/how-to-play-the-fried-liver-attack/)
How do you survive a Fried Liver Attack? There are three ways to successfully defend against the Fried Liver Attack. You can gambit a pawn with 5…Na5 6.Bb5 c6, play the Fritz Variation (5…Nd4), or you can sacrifice your bishop on f2. If you prefer to play positional chess more than tactical positions 5…Na5 is the better option for your playing style.
