The Blackburne-Hartlaub Gambit begins with the moves 1. d4 e5 2. dxe5 d6 3. exd6 Bxd6. This gambit is named after the English chess master Joseph Henry Blackburne and the German player Richard Hartlaub. In this variation, Black sacrifices a pawn in order to rapidly develop the light-squared bishop and open up lines for quick attacking chances.
