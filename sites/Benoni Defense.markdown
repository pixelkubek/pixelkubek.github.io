# Answers from duckduckgo
## [Benoni Defense - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benoni_Defense)
The Old Benoni Defense starts with 1.d4 c5. White usually replies 2.d5 in order to gain space, as 2.dxc5 e6 leads to no advantage, and 2.e4 transposes to the Morra Gambit, also promising equality at best. The Old Benoni may transpose to the Czech Benoni, but there are a few independent variations.

## [Benoni Defense - Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/openings/Benoni-Defense)
The Benoni Defense was first analyzed in 1825 by Aaron Reinganum. Reinganum published a book analyzing responses to the Queen's Gambit and King's Gambit. The book was called (Ben-Oni, or the defenses against the Gambit moves in chess).

## [Benoni Defense: Cormorant Gambit - Openings - Schachzeit](https://www.schachzeit.com/en/openings/benoni-defense/cormorant-gambit)
The Benoni Defense: Cormorant Gambit emerges from the initial moves d4, ♙ c5, ♙ dxc5, and ♙ b6. In this gambit, Black sacrifices the b-pawn to accelerate development and challenge White's control of the center.
